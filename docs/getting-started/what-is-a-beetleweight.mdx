# What is a Beetleweight?

Combat robots are split into many weight classes ranging from tiny 150g "Fairyweights" to the massive 250lb heavyweight beasts you see on Battlebots. **Beetleweights** refer to the **3lb weight category**, which is known to be arguably the most popular and competitive of all weight classes due to its balance of affordability, accessibility, and terrifying power-to-weight ratio due to advances in modern drone power systems (thanks dronebros!).

Beetleweights are fought all around the world - there are countless competitions across North America (check [robotcombatevents.com](https://robotcombatevents.com) or [buildersdb.com](https://buildersdb.com) to find nearby events), but there are also events in South America, Europe (UK beetles are 1500g rather than 3lb), and Asia. So check out your local event and chat with some builders, even if you don't have a bot! In addition to stalking the NHRL discord and reading this document, that's by far the best way to get started.

## Is it hard to get started?

Many people tend to be intimidated by the idea of building a combat robot, but trust me it's really not that bad! Building beetles has gotten so much more accessible in the past few years as many off-the-shelf combat robot-specific components can be bought at a relatively low cost, and there are even full kit robots if you don't want to dive headfirst into a custom design. The community is also as large and helpful as ever, so never be afraid to reach out to people for help!
